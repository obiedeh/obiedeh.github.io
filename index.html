// ABOUT ME

I build operational AI systems for physical and edge environments under real infrastructure constraints.

My background leading nationwide wireless deployment gives me a systems-level view of AI deployment, especially where telemetry, infrastructure reliability, edge inference, and real-time operational constraints matter.

My current work spans Physical AI, edge inference, computer vision, runtime observability, operational safety, and AI-RAN workflows. Across my flagship projects, I work with Jetson-based inference, ROS 2 robotics, urban vision analytics, network telemetry, and time-series forecasting.
